# Example prompts for Invoice2go

- Create an invoice for Mike Torres: 6 hours of electrical work at $85 an hour plus $140 in parts, due in 14 days, and email it to him.
- Turn yesterday's estimate for the Patel kitchen remodel into an invoice and send it.
- Which invoices are more than 30 days overdue? List the client, amount and due date.
- Send a friendly payment reminder to every client with an unpaid invoice from October.
- How much was paid to me this month, and how much is still owed in total?
